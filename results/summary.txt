"Passes" means "passes veraPDF", which is not the same as conforming. ISO 14289-2,
8.2.5.29 requires mathematical expressions to be given as MathML, and veraPDF has no rule
for it: these documents passed before their equations had MathML, and pass now that they
do.

Read against the standard: every clause of section 8 of ISO 14289-2, after most of the
work was done, and the parts of ISO 32000-2 it points to. Checked on the way:
  a paragraph, a table and a footnote that each run over two pages stay one element
  nothing is drawn outside a tag or an artifact in the demo and the thesis sample
  the number of a numbered equation is an Lbl, a footnote has NoteType Footnote, the
    ListNumbering of a list follows its marker or numbering, and the bibliography is a
    Sect with the role doc-bibliography
  with ua-2, a document whose heading levels skip is exported (8.2.5.12 does not ask for
    them to follow on)

Also from that reading: each heading directly in the document opens a Sect that runs to
the next heading of the same or a higher level (8.2.5.5), and the number of a numbered
line is an Artifact structure element at the start of its line (8.3.2). In a document
with two columns over two pages, all 46 numbers are at their own lines.

The wrapper of a figure and its caption in a table cell, a block quote or another figure
is a Sect. Div, Part and NonStruct were tried there and fail (Table 5, TD-Caption and
Aside-Caption, and 8.2.5.27), because they do not count as the caption's parent.

Checked after that: the number of an equation with alt text keeps its label; headings in a
block, columns, a grid and padding all open sections; line numbers end up at their lines
under move, rotate, scale and padding; and loose text with a link in it stays one
paragraph (it was split at the link before, which veraPDF did not object to).

Not done from that reading: a heading inside a table cell, a list item, a quote or a figure
does not open a section.

Reviewed on 22 October, again with Claude, and fixed on the branches: links and bookmarks put
the heading in place of the page (now a go-to action with the page in D and the heading in
SD); a link to something in a page header or footer led to a tag that is never written;
`typst watch` did not make MathML; an equation that could not be converted gave no reason
(now a warning); and a custom contents filler became an artifact in every PDF, not only in
PDF 2.0. veraPDF passed all of these before the fixes.

Not done at all: no screen reader has been tried, on the tags or on the MathML, and
nobody who knows the PDF specification well has reviewed the code.
